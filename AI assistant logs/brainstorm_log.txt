- High-stakes auditable workflows favour constrained patterns like

- Industry routing patterns commonly use a hand-picked confidence

  This is precisely the gap the project fills.

CONFIDENCE MECHANISM EXPLAINED. Yes — it is the neural network situation the user
described. MiniLM-L6 encoder -> pooled embedding (384 dims) -> linear
classification head -> 12 logits -> softmax -> 12 probabilities summing to 1. The
largest is the confidence, native to the architecture; the head is added at
fine-tuning time.

Confirmed the user's confidence/accuracy distinction is exactly what calibration
measures. Raw softmax is systematically overconfident (a network reporting 0.95
may be right 85% of the time), which is why an off-the-shelf 0.8 cutoff is
unsound. Corrected by temperature scaling (one learned scalar dividing the logits)
or isotonic regression, fitted on the 20% calibration split, reported as ECE plus
reliability diagrams before and after.

Noted this is also the operational argument for an encoder over an LLM
classifier: an LLM returns text, so probabilities need logprobs (not always
exposed) or repeated sampling with agreement measurement, whereas the encoder
emits a calibratable distribution natively. Feeds the model-selection deliverable.

WHY THRESHOLDS EXIST AT ALL — classification yields a probability; the system must
yield an action. Converting one to the other requires a cutoff, and "always
auto-reply" is simply a cutoff of zero. The threshold is the hinge between
classifying and acting, and the swept threshold is what produces the
risk-coverage curve. Only the per-class refinement was ornamental.

THE SYSTEM IN ONE PARAGRAPH
A bounded LangGraph agent for IRAS individual income tax email enquiries. It
scrubs PII, classifies the email into one of 12 intent classes with a fine-tuned
MiniLM-L6 encoder, rolls those class probabilities up into 5 routing buckets,
calibrates that confidence with temperature scaling, looks up the governing SOP by
dictionary, and either drafts a reply or escalates with a stated reason. The
contribution is the calibrated escalation mechanism, evaluated as a risk-coverage
curve reported per topic — not the drafting.


CORPUS
- 17 SOPs AUTHORED, not scraped: 14 indexed, 3 held out.
- SOPs are officer-facing HANDLING PROCEDURES, not one-per-website-page.
- IRAS pages fetched widely into .scratch/ (gitignored) as generation input;
  only restatements with URL citations are committed.
- Licensing rests on COPYRIGHT (expression is protected, facts are not), not on
  the IRAS ToU, which read literally also forbids hyperlinking and is not
  observed in practice.
- Confidentiality line: every factual claim traces to a public IRAS URL in
  frontmatter. No real system names, queue ids, codes, SLAs or taxpayer data.
- Every SOP carries a synthetic banner.
- SOP frontmatter is the AGENCY INTEGRATION SEAM: intents: builds CLASS_TO_SOPS,
  auto_reply_permitted/escalate_if builds BUCKET. Escalation policy is
  corpus-derived, not hardcoded.

TAXONOMY — 12 classes, 5 buckets
  auto_answerable ......... filing, tax_reliefs, assessment_and_amendment,
                            payment, residency
  requires_account_lookup . account_specific
  high_consequence ........ hardship_or_waiver, scam_report
  out_of_scope ............ oos_business_tax, oos_other_agency
  no_supporting_sop ....... rental_income*, foreign_income_dta*   (*held out)
- Granularity principle: the COARSEST partition that still lets the drafter
  ground correctly. This is why all reliefs are one class.
- Flags applied after the rollup: computation_requested (lexical, MEASURED),
  multi_intent (2nd-highest class probability), under_specified (KIV).
- account_specific is a class for the prototype but is structurally a flag —
  recorded as a known simplification.

DATASET
- ~1,800 emails, balanced across 12 classes, split 50/20/30.
- Three axes: content, style (incl. Singlish + CFPB-grounded register), season.
- Balance matters under per-topic reporting — no class may be starved.
- Anti-leakage: generator model differs from the classification baseline.

CLASSIFIER AND CONFIDENCE
- MiniLM-L6 (22M) PRIMARY. DistilBERT KIV as a second point on the curve.
- Weights commit to PLAIN GIT (~90MB, under GitHub's 100MB limit). NO git-lfs —
  deliberately, so a grader cloning without lfs cannot get pointer files.
- No Hugging Face Hub dependency; clean-machine run needs no network or account.
- Confidence = softmax over the linear classification head, summed into buckets.
- Calibrated by TEMPERATURE SCALING (one scalar) on the 20% split. Isotonic is
  KIV — ~72 examples per bucket is too thin for a non-parametric fit.
- Encoder over LLM justified operationally: an LLM returns text, so probabilities
  need logprobs or repeated sampling; the encoder emits a calibratable
  distribution natively.

ROUTING
- Retrieval is a DICTIONARY LOOKUP. No vector search, no embeddings, no index.
- Thresholds are PER BUCKET, not per class. Only auto_answerable and

- no_supporting_sop is DERIVED from an empty CLASS_TO_SOPS lookup.

- Escalation reasons: requires_account_lookup, high_consequence, out_of_scope,
